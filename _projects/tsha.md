**TSHA** benchmarks vision-language models on trustworthy indoor safety hazard assessment. Its question-answer data spans images, videos, and panoramas, covering 12 parent hazard groups and 64 fine-grained hazard types.

The benchmark examines whether models can identify and assess risks in indoor environments. It complements scene and data generation by providing safety-oriented tasks for evaluating models that interpret the physical world.
