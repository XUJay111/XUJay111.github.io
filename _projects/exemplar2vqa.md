**Accepted to NeurIPS 2026 · [CCF-A](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/AI/)**

**Exemplar2VQA** generates spatial visual question-answer data in simulated 3D environments. Collaborative agents turn QA exemplars into executable programs, using geometric utilities and simulator feedback to compute grounded answers.

The public implementation contains two connected tracks: camera generation for collecting observations and object metadata, followed by QA generation for producing questions and programmatically computed answers. The resulting synthetic data supports training and evaluation of spatial understanding in vision-language models.
