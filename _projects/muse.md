**MUSE** studies controllable 3D scene authoring as incremental requirement satisfaction. Its memory-grounded multi-agent framework coordinates an Architect, Sculptor, and Inspector to refine scenes while preserving content outside the requested edit.

The work also introduces **AuthorBench**, which evaluates requirement-level controllability and preservation-aware editing. Together, the framework and benchmark support the construction and assessment of controllable scene content for embodied AI environments.
